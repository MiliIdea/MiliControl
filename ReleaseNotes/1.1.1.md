# A dashboard, a living notch, and your desktops in order

- New dashboard at the top of the grid view: clock and date, a month calendar with your events (hover a day to see them), up next from Calendar, a to-do list and a sticky note (Settings ▸ Dashboard).
- A second calendar — Persian (Shamsi), Hijri, Hebrew, Chinese and more — under the date and beside the month.
- To-do list: tick an item off and it fades away after two seconds, with Undo in the meantime.
- Now playing in the notch: when Spotify or Music plays, the notch widens with the artwork and a little wave; hover for title, progress and controls (Settings ▸ Now Playing).
- Messages: unread counts from Telegram, WhatsApp and Slack, and each new message drops down from the notch. With the grid open, the notch lists your unread apps — hover to read, click to go (Settings ▸ Messages).
- Web tabs: open sites like chess.com right inside the grid view from the switcher at its top right; they stay as you left them (Settings ▸ Web Tabs).
- Fullscreen apps and Split View are part of the grid — one native slide away. Leave fullscreen and the tile waits in its slot.
- Rows keep macOS's desktop order, so every slide goes the way you move; reorder in Mission Control and the grid follows.
- Tiles show which browser profile is open ("Google Chrome · Work").
- Four-finger swipes no longer scroll the page under the pointer, and need a shorter movement.
- Automatic updates: MiliControl now keeps itself up to date (Settings ▸ Updates, or Check for Updates… in the menu bar).
