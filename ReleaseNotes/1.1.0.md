- Automatic updates: MiliControl now checks for new versions and can install them for you (Settings ▸ Updates, or Check for Updates… in the menu bar).
- Four-finger swipes no longer scroll the page under the pointer.
- Four-finger swipes need a shorter movement, closer to macOS's own gestures.
- New dashboard at the top of the grid editor: clock and date, a month view with your events (hover a day to see them), up next from Calendar, a to-do list, and a sticky note (Settings ▸ Dashboard).
- Desktops with Chrome, Edge, Brave or Vivaldi show which browser profile is open there ("Google Chrome · Work") with the profile picture.
- Rows keep macOS's desktop order, so every slide goes the way you move. Reorder desktops in Mission Control and the grid follows (Settings ▸ Navigation).
- Fullscreen apps (and Split View) are part of the grid: navigate to them like any desktop, one native slide away. Leave fullscreen and the tile waits in its slot until you come back.
- Now playing in the notch: when Spotify or Music plays, the notch widens with the artwork and a little wave; hover for title, progress and controls (Settings ▸ Now Playing).
- Messages: unread counts from Telegram, WhatsApp and Slack, and each new message briefly in the notch (Settings ▸ Messages).
- With the grid open, the notch's left side shows your unread messaging apps — hover one to read its latest messages, click to go there.
- A second calendar on the dashboard — Persian (Shamsi), Hijri, Hebrew, Chinese and more — shown under the date and beside the month (Settings ▸ Dashboard).
- A to-do list on the dashboard: tick an item off and it fades away after two seconds, with Undo in the meantime.
- Web tabs: open sites like chess.com right inside the grid view from the switcher at its top right; they stay as you left them (Settings ▸ Web Tabs).
